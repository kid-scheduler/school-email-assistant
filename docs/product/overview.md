# Product Overview

## Purpose

School Email Assistant helps parents turn school inbox noise into trusted daily summaries. The first surface may summarize important dates and events; formal calendar candidates are planned for a later stage.

## Core Promise

The product should help a parent know what matters and what requires action without giving automation more authority than it has earned.

## Initial Product Shape

- Connect an inbox.
- Discover likely school senders.
- Let the user approve trusted sources.
- Configure children and topics.
- Process approved-source messages.
- Generate a daily digest.
- Include important dates/events in the digest while deferring formal calendar event candidates to a second product stage.

## Non-Goals For The First Version

- Silent calendar automation.
- Full provider coverage.
- A general-purpose inbox assistant.
- Production billing or organization management.
